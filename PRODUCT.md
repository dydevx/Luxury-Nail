# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Local clients in Burnt Oak and Edgware, primarily browsing on mobile while comparing salons or looking for a convenient way to call, visit, or book an appointment.

## Product Purpose

Present Luxury Nail as a trustworthy, premium neighbourhood salon; communicate services, opening hours, location, and contact details clearly; and convert visits into phone calls and appointment enquiries.

## Brand Personality

Polished, tactile, serene. The experience should feel warmly attentive, feminine without clichés, and confident without becoming showy.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic beauty templates, bright yellow gold, pink-heavy “girly” styling, excessive ornament, glassmorphism, dense card grids, loud animation, and anything that feels bargain-led or synthetic.

## Design Principles

- Let real nail imagery establish quality before copy makes a claim.
- Make calling, booking, directions, and opening times effortless on mobile.
- Build luxury through proportion, material detail, whitespace, and restraint.
- Keep every claim concrete and locally relevant.
- Treat accessibility and performance as part of a professional salon experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and interaction patterns, visible keyboard focus, 44px minimum touch targets, semantic landmarks, useful alt text, reduced-motion support, and a layout that remains readable at 200% zoom.
